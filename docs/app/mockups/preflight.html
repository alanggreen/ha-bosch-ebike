Pre-flight board

The app answers "can I ride?" first. Status opens on one verdict; when the bike moves it turns into the Ride face, and after the ride Status shows the backlog draining. Mockup with synthetic data; use the buttons to change the situation.

Material 3 structure: top app bar, two-destination navigation bar, 48 dp targets, system Back returns to Status. The look is the dashboard's: bone and navy, Barlow Condensed numerals, ruled rows, flip cells for live numbers. A failing SD card or lost bridge raises the orange banner on the Ride screen and turns the Status verdict orange.
